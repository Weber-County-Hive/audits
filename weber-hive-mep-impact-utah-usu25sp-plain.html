What it was for

The federal government and Utah both pay for a program that helps small factories and manufacturers. Utah put in $2.8 million a year. Most of the money went to a nonprofit called iMpact Utah.

What went wrong

After a tip to her hotline, the State Auditor looked at iMpact Utah's records. She found its president used program money for things that did not help the program. In all, she estimates $1.8 million to $2.8 million.

How it happened

The money passed through two state offices before it reached iMpact Utah. Neither office checked how it was spent. Each seems to have thought someone else was in charge of that.

iMpact Utah had no working board to keep watch. It also never filed the yearly money reports the law requires, and no one told the State Auditor it should have.

What happened after

iMpact Utah said it would close at the end of July 2025. All the state agencies agreed to fix their oversight. The state office said it was working with the Attorney General's office on an investigation. As of Oct. 17, 2026, we found no public report of charges or money paid back.

The Legislature still funds the program. Its 2026 budget lists $1.5 million a year for it.

Why it matters to you

This is tax money. A 2026 state audit of the same office used this case as proof of what happens when nobody checks.

The Hive graded the oversight an F. The full case file shows how.
